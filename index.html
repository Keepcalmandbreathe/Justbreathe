What is box breathing?

Box breathing is a simple, highly effective technique to reset your nervous system when things feel overwhelming. By breaking your breath down into four equal parts—inhaling, holding, exhaling, and holding again—it brings your mind back to the present moment and helps settle racing thoughts.

Why take a moment to breathe?

Life moves fast, and it's easy to live in a state of low-key tension without realizing it. Taking just a few minutes out of your day for guided breathing sends a clear signal to your body that it's safe to slow down, lowering stress and clearing out mental clutter instantly.

How the 4-4-4-4 technique works

You don't need any special gear or experience. Just follow the visual rings on your screen through these four simple steps:

  1. Inhale: Breathe in steadily through your nose, filling your lungs completely for four seconds.
  2. Hold: Keep the air comfortably inside your lungs for another four seconds.
  3. Exhale: Release the air smoothly and gently through your mouth over four seconds.
  4. Hold: Pause with empty lungs for four seconds before starting the next round.
